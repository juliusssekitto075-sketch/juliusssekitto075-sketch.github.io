What we do

Services designed around your property needs

From rent collection to student storage, Kaju brings several useful rental services together under one trusted company.

💰

Rent Collection

We collect rent on behalf of landlords, issue payment records and provide clear reporting in line with the agreed management terms.

For landlords
🛠️

Property Rehabilitation

We coordinate repairs and improvements to worn-out properties based on a written scope, approved budget and agreement with the owner.

For property owners
🔧

Property Maintenance

We coordinate routine maintenance, electricity and water bill follow-up, litter collection and security arrangements as agreed.

Property care
📦

Student Holiday Storage

Affordable, organised storage for beds, mattresses, suitcases and other household items while students are away during holidays.

For students
🤝

Tenant Placement

We help landlords advertise vacant units, arrange viewings and connect with prospective tenants. Tenant screening can be arranged with consent.

Low-capital service

Move-in & Move-out Cleaning

About the company

We take care of the details that make rentals work.

Kaju Rental Broker is a property support and rental services company based in Biharwe, Mbarara. We connect landlords, tenants and students with dependable services that make property ownership and renting easier.
